# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes Blinkit grocery sales performance using Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into sales trends, outlet performance, product categories, customer ratings, and outlet distribution.



## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Identify top-performing outlet types.
- Analyze sales by outlet size and location.
- Evaluate customer ratings.
- Discover high-selling product categories.
- Compare Low Fat and Regular product performance.
- Monitor overall sales KPIs.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

### Outlet Performance
- Supermarket Type 1 generated the highest revenue.
- Tier 3 locations contributed the highest sales.
- Medium-sized outlets outperformed other outlet sizes.

### Product Analysis
- Snack Foods and Fruits & Vegetables were among the top-selling categories.
- Low Fat products generated higher sales than Regular products.

### Customer Experience
- Average customer rating remained close to 4.0, indicating positive satisfaction levels.
